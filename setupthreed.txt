==================================================
LIGHTING SETUP
==================================================

Use a professional 3-point/product-render lighting setup.

1. LARGE SOFT KEY LIGHT
- positioned above/front-left
- very large area light
- soft shadows
- high quality illumination

2. SOFT FILL LIGHT
- front/right
- lower intensity
- prevents harsh shadows

3. COOL BLUE RIM / BACK LIGHT
- behind the central stage
- very subtle
- creates blue atmospheric separation

Also use:

- ambient/environment light
- soft contact shadows
- subtle ambient occlusion

Avoid harsh directional shadows.

The future character should receive beautiful soft lighting automatically when placed at the origin.
